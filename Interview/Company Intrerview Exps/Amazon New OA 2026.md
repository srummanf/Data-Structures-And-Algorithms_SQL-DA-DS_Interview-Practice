If you're looking to get hired at Amazon in 2026, here's everything you need to know about the current assessment.

The OA is no longer just "solve two DSA questions and hope for the best." Recent candidates are seeing four different sections, and each tests something different.

1/ DSA: around 40 minutes

Usually one solid LeetCode-style problem under heavy time pressure.

Arrays, binary search, greedy, sorting, trees, graphs and similar patterns can show up. You want to get to a correct approach quickly, write clean code, and cover edge cases without spending half the round figuring out syntax.

2/ Debugging with AI: around 60 minutes

This is probably the biggest change.

You may get an unfamiliar multi-file project with bugs or incomplete behaviour. Your job is to understand the codebase, reproduce the issue, locate the problem, fix it, and get the tests passing.

There is AI assistance inside the environment, so Amazon is also testing whether you can use AI without becoming dependent on it.

Practise reading existing code, following stack traces, navigating larger repos, and debugging something you did not build yourself.

3/ Work Simulation

You get realistic workplace situations through emails or messages and rank possible responses.

Think conflicting deadlines, production bugs, disagreement with teammates, customer impact, or prioritisation.

This is where Amazon's Leadership Principles matter. Ownership, Customer Obsession, Dive Deep, Bias for Action and judgment under pressure all show up here.

4/ Work Style Survey

This is the behavioural part.

You answer questions about how you work, make decisions, collaborate, and handle responsibility. There may not be one perfect answer, but random inconsistency will hurt you.

The bigger takeaway is that Amazon is testing a much wider engineering profile now.

So if Amazon is on your target list, prepare for all four.
Grinding LeetCode alone is no longer enough.
