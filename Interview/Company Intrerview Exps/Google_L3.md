# 💭 Overall Experience

The recruitment process was lengthy and required considerable patience.

Some key observations:

* Multiple periods of silence and occasional interview rescheduling were part of the process.
* Recruitment timelines vary significantly between candidates, making direct comparisons unhelpful.
* Strong communication, clean code, and the ability to discuss trade-offs were just as important as arriving at the optimal solution.
* Team Matching and Hiring Committee reviews may require additional waiting time.

# 📝 Note

> The candidate requested that exact interview questions not be shared.

Most interview problems were presented through unique scenarios or contextual stories rather than standard problem statements. Preparing by understanding fundamental algorithms, data structures, and common problem-solving patterns is likely to be more beneficial than attempting to memorize specific questions.

# 🎯 Key Takeaways

* Build a strong foundation in DSA concepts and patterns.
* Practice explaining approaches and trade-offs while coding.
* Keep code clean and readable.
* Be patient throughout Team Matching and Hiring Committee reviews.
* Avoid comparing interview timelines with those of other candidates.
